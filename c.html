How to order services from the website?

Go to the Products page and click on Buy Now. You'll be asked to create your account by entering your name, email, and password. Once registered, you'll be taken yo your dashboard where you can directly purchase the product you need.

How much do the services cost?

Pricing depends on the type of service and the number of pages required. Final costs will be clearly shared with you before purchase.

How payments work?

We support all kinds of payment. You can choose any kind of payment order while placing your order. You can make payments after your purchase is comfirmed. Incase of any failure in the payment process, please explain the issue to our team immediately.

What involves in making an account?

Firstly, making an account in compulsory for ordering goods. Once an account is created, you can use the same account to order different products frequently.

What involves in delivery?

Once your payment is successful, your order will be processed and prepared according to your requirements. After completion, it will be delivered directly to your dashboard. Delivery time may vary depending on the service selected.

How to create an account?

You can see the Try Notemaker button. Click on it. It will lead you to an interface where you have to enter your nae, email and a suitable password. Once done, click on the submit button. After account verification, your account will be officially registered.
